Road to the Platinum

Platinum: “Vice Got a Price!” · Difficulty 4/10 · About 200 hours · One playthrough, all offline, no online trophies. Roadmap shape: PowerPyx, via GTASpotter.

  1. Step 1 — Story (60–80 hours). Play the story and the optional missions. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter — missions cannot be replayed, and cheats disable trophies and saving.
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist (about 100 hours). After the story, the checklist under Progress > Stats needs 70 of the 80 challenges, every robbery, every underwater discovery, every shooting range, drone race and Bret quest. Most remaining trophies fall out of it.
  3. Step 3 — Cleanup (about 10 hours). Any Strangers & Freaks left and whatever is missing. Once the story is over, every quest giver tied to a trophy comes back.

Watch out: one trophy may be missable — (meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1). Also avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent and makes the police more aggressive. Some quests are Lucia-only (Andy) or Jason-only (Tyrone and Bret).
