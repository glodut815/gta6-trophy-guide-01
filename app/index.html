Don’t miss this one

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy you can miss: meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house during Chapter 1. Follow her out, accept the prompt, and the counter in the Progress menu goes up by 1. You cannot do it again after Chapter 1, so just do it.

Roadmap to the platinum ~200 hours · 1 playthrough · difficulty 4/10

  1. 1

    Story & side missions 60–80 h

    Play through the story and pick up optional missions as they appear: purple icons, plus black icons with pink letters. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars — 100% needs 200 of the 277 vehicle models and 100 of the 255 animals. Everything you do before the ending already counts toward the checklist.

  2. 2

    The 100% checklist & Challenges ~100 h

    After the ending, Progress > Stats gains a 100% Completion entry. Most side quests are not required and collectibles count only in part. You do need every robbery, every underwater discovery, every shooting range, every drone race and the Bret quest. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so skip the nastiest ones.

  3. 3

    Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 h

    The checklist does not ask for these quests, so they are usually what is left afterwards, together with any stray trophies that did not pop on their own. Finish the eight stranger strands, then sweep the unconfirmed hidden trophies and the last few collectibles.

Keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter (10 slots; the game gives you 15 manual slots plus one autosave). Cheats unlock only after the story and disable both trophies and saving — keep them off your platinum save.
