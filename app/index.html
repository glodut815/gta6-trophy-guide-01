Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough, all of it offline, and there is no mission replay — so keep a save at the start and the end of every chapter. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and puts it at about 200 hours.

  1. Step 1 — Story and side missions 60–80 hours

    Play through the story and pick up optional missions as they appear: purple icons, plus black icons with pink letters. Along the way, scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars. 100% needs 200 of the 277 base vehicle models and 100 of the 255 animals — studying is enough, you do not have to hunt them. Everything you do before the ending already counts toward the checklist.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist and Challenges about 100 hours

    After the ending, Progress, then Stats, gains a 100% Completion entry. Most side quests are not required, and collectibles only partly. You do need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and the Bret quest. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so you can skip the nastiest ones.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    The checklist does not ask for these quests, so they are usually what is left afterwards, together with any stray trophies that did not pop on their own. Finish the eight Strangers & Freaks questlines and sweep up the rest with the checklist filters.

Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off the save you are using for the platinum. There are no difficulty settings, and no trophy needs online play.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy can be missed. Everything else is still earnable in free roam after the story ends.

Five trophies still unsolved

These hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet. Their guides say so plainly, and this guide will be updated as players pin them down.

Watch out

  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. They only unlock after the story, which is exactly when you will be mopping up most trophies — keep them off your platinum save.
  • No mission replay. Keep a separate save at the start and end of every chapter; the game gives you 15 manual slots and one autosave.
  • Some optional missions expire and a few fences disappear for good, but every character tied to a trophy is still around in free roam after the ending.
  • Do not pick the Irredeemable criminal profile. It is permanent and makes the police more aggressive.
