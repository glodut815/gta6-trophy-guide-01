    Roadmap to the Platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough is enough (PowerPyx). Work the stages in order: the story first, then strangers, activities, collectibles, and crime last. The 100% checklist (A Long Road) comes after the credits — its thresholds are lenient, roughly 60% of true completion.

      Missables — only one is flagged

      PowerPyx and GTABase both flag Positive Mental Attitude as the only trophy that may be missable: the first meditation, Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, disappears after that chapter. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. One more timed thing to watch: the Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and counts toward Strangers & Freaks — do it the moment it appears.
